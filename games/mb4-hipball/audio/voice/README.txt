synthesized horn-stab + crowd-roar stinger.

  fight.mp3         start of every rally

WHERE TO FIND VOICE CLIPS:

1. VoiceBosch's "Mr. Happy" pack on OpenGameArt/itch.io is a high-energy
   fighting-game announcer with the right NBA Jam-ish vibe.
     https://opengameart.org/content/mr-happy-announcer-audio-pack
   Licensed CC-BY-SA 4.0. The pack contains generic phrases like "Fight!",
   "Victory!", "You Win!" — perfect for the game events above. But it does
   NOT contain the specific powerup names. For those you need:

2. Record them yourself. Open your phone's voice memo, shout each name
   with maximum NBA-Jam-announcer gusto. Quick-trim. Export as MP3. Drop
   in. It will sound BETTER than any free voice pack because it has the
   right energy for your specific names.

3. Hire a voice actor — Fiverr has $5-15 announcers who'll do all 8
   powerup names in one take.

If you don't add ANY clips, the game just plays the synth stinger
(horn stab + crowd roar) which sounds intentional and arcade-y — way
better than the previous robotic TTS.
