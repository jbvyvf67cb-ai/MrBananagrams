A Legacy in Every Bounce

You journeyed 3,500 years — from the Olmec inventors of rubber, through the Spanish conquest, across two centuries of European bewilderment, into the Amazon rubber boom, and onward to the modern world.

Every bouncing ball you've ever held is a thread back to a Mesoamerican laboratory in 1600 BCE. Brilliant science happens everywhere — and ideas travel, though the exchange is rarely fair.
